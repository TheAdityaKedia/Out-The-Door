# Changelog

## 2026-11-12: first model (body-1, cap-1, drum-1, pin-1)
Started from Doorstep body-4 / drum-3 at Adi's request: no front plate, board loads from the back, simple back closure.
- Front: closed 2 mm wall with one convex bevel (t^1.6 profile) from a 72 x 36 outline down to a window 0.3 mm inside the live area. 1 mm flat band inside the 3 mm edge round.
- Board: stops on 8 pads (0.75 mm over its bare front rim, 45° undersides so they print without supports). 4 snap arms (1.0 mm thick, 11 mm long, 0.93% strain) catch its back edge with 0.12 mm preload. Doorstep's edge stops, far-end ribs and wheel-end blocks/press tabs kept. Corner front snaps, ledges, leaf springs, seat and groove dropped.
- Drum post: hole now goes through; the pin goes in from the back with its head behind the post. The post is flattened on the board side (x 31.95) so the panel's wheel slides past it as the board goes in.
- Back: first tried an inset plate in a rabbet; the opening it needs left only 70 mm² of back rim to print on. Switched to a full back cap split at the start of the back edge round (world y = DB-4): 394 mm² of rim on the bed. Cap has 1.0 mm lips 3.5 mm deep (0.1 mm off the wall) and 4 snap tabs whose 0.45 mm hooks click into pockets in the walls (wall stays 1.2 mm there). Pry notch under the seam at the bottom.
- Fit checks: no clashes with the panel apart from the hub on the wheel (by design) and the snap preload; board, drum, pin and cap all have clear insertion paths.
